# P10.1 — Front camera spike on the Fire HD 10 Plus

Side: build   Tier: deep   Depends on: none   Timebox: 2 working days
Verification: hardware
Issue: #88
Status: ready

## Why

Camera presence (Phase 10) only makes sense if the Office Kiosk can run its front camera continuously, on the device, at a CPU and battery cost a wall tablet can carry. None of that is known. This spike answers it with measurements and ends in a go or no-go ADR before any presence code is written. It needs no spec input: it measures the device, not behavior.

## Questions to answer, with evidence

1. **Access.** Can the app open the front camera on Fire OS (API 28) from its activity, and from its foreground service while another app or the screensaver is in front? Does it keep the camera with the panel off (night power-off, F-SCR-14)? What does Fire OS show the user while the camera runs (an indicator, a notification, nothing)?
2. **Permission.** Does `pm grant net.lockney.tabula android.permission.CAMERA` over ADB work, or does it need the on-device tap? Record the provisioning step for DEVICE.md.
3. **Frames.** The smallest resolution and lowest frame rate the front camera delivers through CameraX or Camera2; exposure behavior in a dim room (the night-mode case).
4. **Detectors that need no Google services** (Fire OS has none). Try at least: frame differencing on a downscaled luminance plane (motion), and one face option among `android.media.FaceDetector`, Camera2 hardware face statistics (`STATISTICS_FACE_DETECT_MODE`) or a bundled model. For a bundled model, record its license; it must be compatible with Apache-2.0 distribution.
5. **Cost.** CPU (`dumpsys cpuinfo`, `top`) and battery drain (`dumpsys batterystats`, on and off the charger) for each detector at two or three frame rates, with the dashboard idle behind the clock screensaver. Compare against the Phase 4 idle target (< 15% CPU) and the device's heat. Note thermal throttling if seen.
6. **Range.** Rough detection distance and angle in the office for motion and for a face, at day and night light.

## Do

1. A throwaway debug build path (a debug-only activity or flavor, not shipped in release builds) that runs each detector and logs results and timings.
2. A script under `tools/device/` that collects the cost numbers, so the run is repeatable on the second Fire HD.
3. An ADR: go or no-go, the chosen camera API and detector(s), the frame rate and resolution budget, what runs when (screensaver only, panel off, always), privacy commitments (frames stay in memory on the device, nothing stored or sent, only a presence state leaves), and the provisioning step. On no-go, the plan's Phase 10 keeps P10.2 (HA presence) and closes P10.3 and P10.4 as not planned.
4. Delete the throwaway code paths, or keep them behind a debug-only flag named in the ADR.

## Out of scope

- Production presence logic, settings, entities (P10.2 to P10.4).
- Camera views, streaming, snapshots.

## Done when

- The ADR exists with pasted device output for every question above (the hardware verifier pastes what the executor cannot run).
- `make check` passes.

## Hand back

Result section: the measurements table, the go/no-go, anything that changes P10.2 to P10.4. If the timebox runs out, record what was answered and hand back.
