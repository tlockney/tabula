Side: build   Tier: standard   Depends on: P2.1, P1.4
Verification: hardware
Issue: #18
Status: awaiting-hardware

## Why

The kiosk idles to a clock instead of a static dashboard, and HA automations can start and dismiss it.

## Do

1. Idle timer and a Compose overlay with Black and Digital Clock modes.
2. Dismiss on touch, an external HA trigger to dismiss, and the next-screensaver sensor.
3. Timings from ADR 0002.
4. Write the hardware check as a script in `tools/device/`.

## Result

### What was built

- `android/core`:
  - `ScreensaverController` (`core.screensaver`, plain JVM): the idle timer in wall-clock time with nested pauses, touch and command start and stop, the master switch, the clock stand-in for modes not built yet, the backlight level while it shows, and `next_screensaver` in whole seconds.
  - `StateDelta.NO_NEXT_SCREENSAVER` and its serializer send `next_screensaver: null` (ADR 0013 item 5).
- `android/ha`: `TlpClient` sends device events (`tabula/event`) from a channel once the session is registered.
- `android/app`:
  - `ScreensaverManager`: runs the timer against the clock and carries out each outcome (overlay, backlight hold, events, state).
  - `ScreensaverView`: the overlay in plain views: a digital clock with the date, black, and dim (nothing drawn).
  - `ScreenManager.setOverride`: holds the panel at the screensaver level and restores the level decided meanwhile (F-SSV-17).
  - `MainActivity` reports touches and keys and whether its window is in front. `TabulaCommands` handles `screensaver`. The service declares the `screensaver` capability and reports its state at registration.
- Docs and scripts:
  - ADR 0013 and the ADR 0002 `SCREENSAVER_EVENT_BUFFER` constant.
  - DEVICE.md hardware check section, `tools/device/check_p24.sh`, and `tools/device/ha_events.py` (records HA events for the check).
  - PROVENANCE rows.
- Deviation from "Do" item 1: the overlay uses plain framework views, not Compose (ADR 0013 item 1).
- Not built (ADR 0013 follow-ups): dismissal when another app takes the foreground, HA entities for the master switch and idle timeout, clock faces and options, a separate dim level, the postpone action, waking the panel when the screensaver stops while it is off (F-SSV-20), and the Phase 3 modes.
