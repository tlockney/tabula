# P10.0 — Spec: camera-based presence

Side: spec   Tier: standard   Depends on: none
Verification: review
Issue: #87
Status: in-review

This is a spec-side packet. A context that executes it must not write code in this repository afterwards.

## Why

Plan section 4 now brings the tablet's own camera into scope as a presence sensor (Phase 10). `docs/FEATURES.md` lists camera motion and face wake only as deferred or dropped hooks, so the build side has no behavior to build from. This packet adds that behavior to `docs/FEATURES.md`.

Thomas's request (2026-11-21), the design input from the project owner: "Go ahead and add camera-based detection to the plan."

## Result

**Spec written.** Thirteen entries: twelve in a new area, Presence (F-PRS), and one event entry in F-EVT. The area table gains F-PRS and the totals move from 255 to 268 entries; F-EVT goes from 7 to 8. F-SSV-24 remains the HA source and now points at the presence area; presence, not the camera, is the abstraction the rest of the file cites.

**New IDs**

| ID | One line |
| --- | --- |
| F-PRS-01 | One presence rule fed by an HA sensor and by the tablet's camera; each source has its own switch, and the rule is satisfied while any enabled source reports someone there. |
| F-PRS-02 | The camera's two detectors: motion (works in the dark, discards whole-frame lighting changes) and face (reacts only when a person faces the tablet, needs light); motion takes precedence, and each has its own sensitivity setting. |
| F-PRS-03 | A presence report dismisses a running screensaver and restarts the idle countdown once the source clears; a source already present when the screensaver starts does not dismiss it until it clears and returns. |
| F-PRS-04 | Option to act only after the panel has gone off, shared with the HA source, with what counts as off spelled out. |
| F-PRS-05 | Option to hold the idle countdown while presence continues, which needs dismissal on for the same source and keeps the camera watching. |
| F-PRS-06 | Camera lifetime, faults and cost: off by default, running only while a rule needs it, released at once, treating a busy or silent camera as a fault, and a frame-rate setting left to the architect. |
| F-PRS-07 | Privacy: analysis on the device, frames in memory only, nothing stored, logged, uploaded or backed up, no identification, and the user shown that the camera is running. |
| F-PRS-08 | Enabling and consent: a person at the tablet or an ADB grant, the missing and withdrawn permission states, and the fall back to the other sources. |
| F-PRS-09 | What HA sees: a presence binary sensor, a camera-active binary sensor, the controls, and entity presence following the hardware. |
| F-PRS-10 | Lockdown: presence neither dismisses, postpones nor wakes, with an open question on whether reporting continues. |
| F-PRS-11 | Schedule slots can switch a detector off for their window, which also stops the camera when nothing else needs it; overrides only remove. |
| F-PRS-12 | Limits: another app covering the screen, and hardware without a usable camera. |
| F-EVT-08 | `tabula_presence`: emitted on a change of the answer, carrying the new state, the source and whether it dismissed or postponed the screensaver (independent design). |

**Deferred lines moved.** These lines changed in the "Deferred and dropped" section:

- "Camera, camera snapshot, motion/person sensors, camera views, RTSP: deferred." is now "Camera snapshot, camera views, camera streams and RTSP: deferred", with a pointer to the presence area. Motion and face sensing moved into the spec.
- "Camera motion, face and proximity events: dropped with the device camera" is gone; the existing proximity line still covers proximity and hardware person sensors.
- "Camera-stream screensaver and camera-based motion/face wake: deferred with camera work." now defers only the camera-stream screensaver.
- "Pausing the device's own camera / camera views behind the screensaver: device camera dropped." is gone; the camera's lifetime is F-PRS-06.
- "Camera/microphone/nearby-devices/log-access/all-files permissions" now names microphone, nearby-devices, log-access and all-files only; the camera grant moved into the kiosk area's permission list as a presence-only grant (F-PRS-08).
- Camera hand gestures, camera views, snaps, streams and RTSP stay deferred, unchanged.

Three entries outside the deferred list were adjusted so the file keeps one vocabulary: the kiosk permission list (camera now a presence grant), the dashboard-link tap rule (points at F-PRS-08 rather than saying such features do not exist yet), and the screensaver entries that name presence as a dismissal, schedule override and wake source (F-SSV-04, F-SSV-18, F-SSV-20). The F-SSV-24 "Open" note on deferred device sensors now points at the presence area and keeps only proximity and hardware person sensors deferred.

**Marked independent design.** Three items have no counterpart in the source material and are labelled so in the file: that the master switch cannot be turned on from HA alone (a person at the tablet or an ADB grant), that the tablet shows a sign while the camera is watching, and the presence event with its payload. The privacy rules and the camera-active indicator come from the plan's Phase 10 constraints, not from the source.

**Open questions for the architect**

1. Whether two sources combine as "any reports presence", or one takes over from the other while it reports.
2. Whether the face detector's distance setting is one control or two (near and far), and what each default is.
3. Whether the "only after the panel goes off" option applies to every source at once or can be set per source.
4. Whether the camera can keep watching while another app covers the screen or the panel is truly off; the plan's spike decides this and it may differ per device type.
5. What the on-screen sign looks like, where it sits and how long it stays, and whether a small live preview of the detector's view should serve as it.
6. Whether a camera grant made over ADB is reflected back to HA as the switch coming on.
7. Whether presence reporting to HA continues under lockdown.
8. Whether sensitivity can be set per schedule slot, or only on and off.
9. Whether a change caused by several sources at once yields one presence event or one per source.

**Review findings**

A second reviewer applied the P0.2r method to the thirteen new entries and the edited lines: phrase-overlap scans at seven and eight words, a numeral scan, a framing scan, a behavior-by-behavior coverage check of the source's camera and presence material, and a read of plan sections 4 and 7.6 against the independent-design claims. Four findings, all resolved here.

| Entry | Type | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F-PRS-01, F-PRS-03, F-PRS-09 | gap | Nothing said how long a camera source keeps reporting after the trigger stops, so "the source clears" had no meaning for a camera and a single movement could appear to hold the sensor on indefinitely. | F-PRS-01 now defines a hold window on camera detections (constant: architect picks) and makes the rest of the file read that held state: the countdown restart in F-PRS-03, the postpone rule in F-PRS-05 and the sensor in F-PRS-09 all point at it. An Open line covers whether the window is per camera or per detector and what its default should be. |
| F-PRS-02 | clarity | "Where motion evidence already exists" could be read as requiring the motion detector's switch to be on, which contradicts a face-only setup the same entry allows. | Split into two statements: the cheap change detection is the gate for the face model and runs regardless of whether motion is a presence trigger, while motion only wins as the trigger when both are enabled. Added the visible consequence for someone who holds still, and an Open line on whether a slow re-ask is worth its cost. |
| F-PRS-07 | gap (minor) | The brief live view of the analysed frames, used for aiming and tuning, existed only inside the open question about the on-screen sign and could vanish when that question is settled. | It now has its own Open line, stating that resolving the sign must not drop the aid, and a one-line hook in the deferred list so it cannot disappear quietly. |
| F-PRS-02 | paraphrase | The two examples of a discarded whole-frame change repeated the source's pair in the source's order. | Replaced with different examples (an exposure shift, a large colour wash) so the entry shares no phrasing with the source. |

The reviewer's mechanical checks were clean before these changes and remain so after them: no constants, no seven- or eight-word overlap with the source material in the new entries, count reconciliation at 268 / 12 / 8, and the three independent-design labels holding.
