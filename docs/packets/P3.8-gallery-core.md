# P3.8 — Gallery core (photo slideshow)

Side: build   Tier: deep   Depends on: P3.1
Verification: hardware
Issue: #66
Status: awaiting-hardware

## Why

F-SSV-12's photo slideshow, minus the HA media-browser source: a folder on the device, photos picked with the system picker (copied into app storage), and a single image URL fetched fresh per slide. Transitions, fit modes, panel-resolution decode with prompt release, edge-tap zones and the EXIF details overlay. Deep because the image pipeline is the most memory-sensitive code in the app and the target is a low-RAM tablet.

Unverified device fact: nothing has measured decode memory or GC pressure on the HD 10 Plus (the Phase 2 hardware checks #15–#20 have not run). The hardware script therefore includes `dumpsys meminfo` assertions alongside the visual checks; no separate spike is needed, but the JVM tests must cover the release/reuse logic.

## Do

1. Playlist built when the screensaver starts (new files appear next time); shuffle and include-subfolders for the folder source; the picker source copies into app storage (no broad storage permission — SAF, and the copy survives reboots). The `media_browser` source is in the schema from P3.1 but served by P3.9; until that packet lands, `source: media_browser` behaves as an unbuilt source and the ADR 0013 item-3 clock stand-in shows.
2. Transitions (none, crossfade, slide, zoom, slow pan-and-zoom for stills, random per slide) and fit (letterbox, smart fill with a blurred darkened enlargement of the photo filling the bars when shapes differ a lot, always fill).
3. Decode at panel resolution; the previous slide's bitmap is released before the next is shown (an explicit decodable invariant in the JVM tests with fake bitmaps).
4. The URL source: the current photo stays until the next has loaded; a failed fetch keeps it and retries later; a small history of recent photos allows stepping back.
5. Edge zones: a tap near one side edge goes back one slide, near the other goes forward, and the interval restarts; elsewhere a tap dismisses (F-SSV-04's exception).
6. EXIF details overlay (date taken, camera details; no location lookup), placed in a free corner, displaced by corner widgets when those land (F-WGT-02).
7. JVM tests: playlist build (shuffle determinism injectable), transition/fit selection, decode/release lifecycle, URL loader (fake transport: failure keeps current, history, fresh-fetch-per-slide), edge-tap zoning.
8. `tools/device/check_p38.sh` (visual checks plus `dumpsys meminfo` before/after N slides) and a DEVICE.md section, including how the test photos get onto the device (ADB push into app storage or the picker).
9. `PROVENANCE.md` rows.

### Deviations / design choices (recorded in ADR 0016)

- The picker is launched from the service's ongoing notification action: the
  kiosk has no device-side settings UI to hang it on.
- Empty/unbuildable playlists (including `media_browser` until P3.9) show the
  ADR 0013 item-3 clock stand-in; lifecycle events keep reporting Photos.
- A failed fetch or decode keeps the current photo and moves the next
  attempt one full interval away (no hot retry loop); a failed decode moves
  to the next photo at that attempt.
- The transition-timed release lands `GALLERY_TRANSITION_MS +
  GALLERY_RELEASE_MARGIN_MS` after the show (new constant): the view clears
  the old ImageView at the animation's end and the margin covers the jitter,
  so a recycled bitmap is never drawn.
- Gallery settings apply when the slideshow starts; live mid-run
  reconfiguration is left to the schedule packet (F-SSV-18).

### Review round (PR #76, "changes requested")

Both reviews' musts, shoulds and nits are addressed (ADR 0016 records the
resulting semantics):

1. **Stale end action wiping the new slide (must).** `GalleryView.draw` now
   cancels both layers' animations before placing anything — a cancelled
   animator never runs its pending end action — and resets the outgoing
   layer to the settled state (alpha, scale, translation) before animating
   it out. Pinned on the JVM by the engine tests below (the view is not
   JVM-testable) and on device by a new `check_p38.sh` step: two edge taps
   inside one transition window, asserting both steps land and no crash.
2. **Release before draw with `none`, and the posted-draw-after-stop race
   (must).** The render is now synchronous (the carry-out already runs on
   Main.immediate), the engine applies a outcome's releases only after its
   shows have rendered, and `draw` guards on `isRecycled` as well. Pinned by
   `with no transition the new slide renders before the replaced one is
   released` (an event-order log: render, render, release).
3. **Superseded and cancelled decodes leaked bitmaps (must).** The engine
   releases a superseded decode's handle at the generation drop; a decode
   cancelled mid-`BitmapFactory` recycles its own bitmap when the coroutine
   is no longer active on exit. Pinned by both supersede tests, which now
   assert the stale bitmap is released, and by the mutation check.
4. **The two-bitmap bound under rapid taps (should).** A show flushes the
   releases still pending from an earlier transition (its draw has cleared
   their layers). Pinned by `shows inside one transition flush the replaced
   slides' release, keeping the two-bitmap bound`.
5. **Main-thread folder walk (should).** The listing (and the URL ring reset)
   moved into `withContext(Dispatchers.IO)` in `GalleryView.show`, now a
   suspend function called from the screensaver collector.
6. **Ring/history drift (nit).** `UrlPhotoFetcher` now keeps a ring of the
   successful fetches' files and deletes the file that falls out of that
   ring — the same one the controller's history evicts; a failed fetch
   consumes nothing.
7. **Generation guard untested (nit).** New test `a superseded decode is
   released and never rendered while its replacement still loads`: both
   decodes gated, the stale one answers while the replacement still loads.
   Mutation-checked: removing the generation guard from `loadDecode` fails
   it (and the older supersede test); restored and re-run green.
8. **Panel-off pause (nit).** `GalleryEngine.setActive` parks the timer; the
   activity feeds it `TabulaStatus.foreground` (a powered-off panel stops the
   activity, so the window signal covers both F-SSV-02 cases). Pinned by
   `the slideshow pauses while Tabula is not in front and resumes where it
   was`.
9. **ADR 0002 placement (nit).** The gallery constants section moved out of
   the middle of the schema-v2 section, to beside "Notifications and media".
10. **All-undecodable folder (nit).** A file playlist where every photo has
    failed with nothing ever shown reports a dead end; the view hides and
    the clock stand-in shows (ADR 0016 item 1). Pinned by `a folder whose
    photos all fail to decode falls back to the clock stand-in`.
11. **check_p38.sh header (nit).** The cleanup claims now match the trap:
    switch off, listener stopped, nothing else touched; plus the
    rapid-tap step from (1).
12. **ADR 0016 sentence (nit).** Item 4 explains why the timed release is
    safe: synchronous render-before-release, cancel-on-draw, and the
    show-time flush of pending releases.
