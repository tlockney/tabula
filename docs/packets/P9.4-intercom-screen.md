# P9.4 — Intercom screen and ringing UI

Side: build   Tier: standard   Depends on: P9.3
Verification: hardware
Issue: #142
Status: ready

## Why

A call needs a person-facing surface: choose who to call, see an incoming call, answer or decline, see who is connected for the whole call, and hang up. ADR 0025 decision 6 describes the screen; ADR 0028 requires that the screen always shows who is connected.

## Do

1. An intercom screen: lists the other intercom-capable devices by HA area name (device name when no area is set), plus a Broadcast button. The device list comes from HA through the existing channel; add the minimal TLP or settings plumbing only if none exists, and record it in the Result. Devices in the caller's own area are not listed for two-way calls (ADR 0025).
2. Incoming ring UI: full-screen over the dashboard and over a screensaver, wakes the panel, shows caller name and mode, Answer and Decline, stops at the ring limit. A broadcast receiver shows who is speaking and a Reply button that places a two-way call back.
3. In-call UI: always shows who is connected, call duration and remaining time to the hard limit, a Hang up button, and "cannot talk back" on a listen-only device. The banner stays while a call is up, including over a screensaver.
4. Action registry: actions to open the intercom screen and to call a fixed target (device or area) so gestures, the side menu and dashboard links can use them (ADR 0025 decision 6).
5. Compose or view tests against the fake engine: every state renders, buttons call the right engine methods, the banner persists, listen-only wording. Screenshots of each state from the desk or an emulator without audio are welcome in the Result.
6. `docs/provenance/P9.4.md`: independent design.

## Out of scope

The engine (P9.3), auto-answer (P9.5), voice-started calls (P9.6).

## Done when

- `make check` and the Android unit-test target pass; output pasted.
- Device part (hardware verifier, owner-scheduled): ring, answer, decline and hang up between two kiosks. Set `awaiting-hardware`.

## Hand back
