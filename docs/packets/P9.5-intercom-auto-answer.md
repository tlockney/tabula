# P9.5 — Intercom auto-answer settings

Side: build   Tier: standard   Depends on: P9.3
Verification: hardware
Issue: #143
Status: awaiting-hardware

## Why

Hands-free reaching a person is the reason intercom exists (ADR 0025), and that needs a tablet that answers itself. It is also the riskiest part: a microphone opens without a tap. ADR 0028 narrowed the rule after the house heard feedback: auto-answer may only answer a call that a person or a schedule asked for, and the default stays off.

### Gaps (recorded, not worked around)

1. **A ring carries no origin.** Item 2's "never when the ring did not come from an explicit HA service call or device-screen request; a ring carries its origin so the device can check" cannot be implemented against the merged protocol: `intercom_ring` (PROTOCOL 4.5) is `call_id`, `caller_device_id`, `caller_name`, `mode`. Guard 1 holds in practice for now - the integration rings only for a request it was asked to make, so every v1 ring is an explicit request - but the device cannot tell. Proposal for P9.1b, additive to ADR 0037's `intercom_start`/`peer_device_id` (open PR #174, which does not carry a ring origin): an optional `origin` (`service` | `device` | `schedule`) on `intercom_ring`, absent meaning `service` so older integrations keep working, and the device refusing an automatic answer unless the origin is `service` or `device`. No protocol file was changed here.
2. **The in-call banner is P9.4's and P9.4 is not merged.** An auto-answered call reports `in_call` and opens audio exactly like an answered one; nothing draws it yet. Item 2's banner requirement lands with P9.4, which must cover the auto-answered case (there was no tap to bring the screen up).
3. **`auto_answer_from` holds Tabula device ids, not HA registry ids.** The ring's `caller_device_id` is the only caller identity the device has (PROTOCOL 4.5), so the list has to match it; the options-flow editor therefore takes ids one per line. A picker by device name needs the panel.
4. **The tone's audibility is the hardware part.** The engine guarantees the order (tone, then the connection); that the beep is audible at the volume the household set, and that the far end does not hear its tail through the fresh capture, can only be heard on a device.

The two P9.2 open questions do not block this packet: (1) is about a *caller's* engine for an HA-started call, and auto-answer runs on the callee, which always receives the call id in its ring; (2) is stale state after an HA restart, which P9.3's boot and limit guards already answer. No protocol change was made and none is needed for the CI part.

No new ADR: the schema bump and its down-translation follow ADR 0030 decision 2, and the constants were recorded in ADR 0002 rather than invented in code.

### Device part (owner-scheduled, hardware verifier)

1. Two tablets at schema 4, both with the microphone granted. On tablet A set `intercom_auto_answer_from` to B's device id and `auto_answer` to `listed`; on B leave `off`.
2. From A, call B: B plays the tone, answers with no tap, and audio flows both ways; the far end hears the tone before B's room.
3. From B, call A (A is `listed` for B's id, B's own setting is `off`): A answers; B's own ring does not auto-answer itself, since it is the caller.
4. Set B to `listed` with A *not* in the list, call B: B rings for the whole `INTERCOM_RING_LIMIT_S` and never answers; the call ends with `timeout`.
5. Decline a ring on B while the tone is still in the window (a manual answer and a decline both): nothing answers, no microphone is left open, `intercom_declined`/`intercom_ended` arrive.
6. With the microphone permission revoked on B, call B: B rings (listen-only) and never auto-answers.
7. `tabula.intercom_end` from HA during an auto-answered call: both sides end at once, no microphone stays open. After it, watch for `intercom_state` idle.

## Review brief

Two independent reviews are needed, because the code opens a microphone with no tap and it touches WebRTC:

- **Audio review:** `CallMachine.ring`/`beginAutoAnswer`/`onAnswerTone`, `CallTone`, `AndroidCallTone` (`TONE_PROP_BEEP` on `STREAM_VOICE_CALL`, generator release, volume 100 of the stream), and whether every cancellation path (decline, HA end, tap, ring limit, transport loss) really closes the window before `answer()`. Check too that a broadcast and a listen-only device keep the record path closed.
- **WebRTC review:** that an auto-answer is the ordinary `answer()` path (offer handling, `pendingOffer`, `pendingCandidates`, connect timer, ICE), that nothing new runs off the machine's monitor, and that `AndroidCallTone` cannot block or throw into the engine.

Head commit and the CI evidence are in this file's Result; `make check` and `make android-test` are both green at that commit.
