# P1.4 — Commands and services

Side: build   Tier: standard   Depends on: P1.3
Verification: ci
Issue: #12
Status: done

## Why

HA needs to drive devices: notifications, TTS, screensaver, brightness. Commands carry a `command_id` and are ACKed so failures surface in automations.

## Read first

- `AGENTS.md`
- `docs/PLAN.md` sections 5.5 and 7.2 (P1.4)
- `docs/PROTOCOL.md`
- `docs/decisions/0001-ha-platform.md`, `docs/decisions/0002-constants.md`
- `integration/custom_components/tabula/`

## Do

1. Command dispatch with `command_id`, result awaiting and timeouts.
2. `tabula.*` services (entity-targeted) and the standard-platform service handlers.
3. Brightness ownership rule (plan 5.5).

## Out of scope

- Settings (P1.5).

## Done when

- `make test` passes, with tests for: `notify.send_message` and `tabula.notify` reach the mock and are ACKed; a NACK or timeout raises `HomeAssistantError`; `tts.speak` targeting the media_player sends `play_media` with a URL the mock can fetch with its credential; a manual brightness change turns adaptive off.
- `make check` passes.
- `PROVENANCE.md` updated.
