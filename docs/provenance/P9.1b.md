# P9.1b: The caller's own frame and per-peer signaling

| Behavior | Source | Packet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A call started from Home Assistant now rings and can be answered: the tablet that placed it is told which call it is in and who it is calling, so its own side opens instead of staying idle while the callee rings | independent design, ADR 0037 decision 2 | P9.1b |
| A broadcast now carries audio to each tablet it reaches: one connection per receiver, each offer and answer delivered only to the device it belongs to | independent design, ADR 0037 decisions 1 and 4 | P9.1b |
| A tablet that cannot act as the calling end of a call HA started is refused that call with a message instead of being rung with nothing to connect to | independent design, ADR 0037 decisions 8 and 9 | P9.1b |
| A call or broadcast a tablet asked for from its own screen that HA refuses now stops on that tablet instead of sitting in a call with nothing carrying it | independent design, ADR 0037 decision 10 | P9.1b |
| A broadcast that would reach more tablets than one call carries is refused and names them, rather than silently reaching fewer | independent design, ADR 0037 decision 7 | P9.1b |
| A tablet that can be rung and can answer keeps working unchanged, with no update needed to receive a broadcast | independent design, ADR 0037 decision 8 | P9.1b |
