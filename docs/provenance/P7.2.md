# P7.2: Voice protocol and mock-device voice capability

| Behavior | Source | Packet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A tablet that can capture audio announces a `voice` capability when it registers, so HA offers it voice features and a tablet that cannot capture never sees them | independent design, ADR 0024 item 2, ADR 0033 item 1 | P7.2 |
| While HA is listening to the tablet, the tablet reports whether it is idle or listening, so an automation or dashboard can tell that the microphone is open | independent design, ADR 0033 item 5 | P7.2 |
| A voice turn ends by itself after five minutes even if HA never says stop, so a dropped command cannot leave a tablet's microphone open | independent design, ADR 0033 item 4 | P7.2 |
