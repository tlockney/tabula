# P9.0: Intercom spike

Rows for the behaviors this packet measured and that later Phase 9 packets build
on. The spike app itself is debug-only and adds nothing a user sees.

| Behavior | Source | Packet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two Tabula devices play two-way audio directly between themselves on one LAN, with no server in the audio path | independent design (ADR 0025, ADR 0028) | P9.0 |
| A Tabula device without a usable microphone still receives and plays a call, and sends no audio | independent design (ADR 0025 decision 7, ADR 0028) | P9.0 |
| A call starts or answers only when a person asks for it or a schedule does; a device never answers because it is online (the auto-answer default stays off) | independent design (ADR 0025 decision 1, narrowed by ADR 0028 after the feedback finding) | P9.0 |
| Every intercom call ends on its own at a fixed maximum, and HA can end a call in any state, including one that is feeding back | independent design (ADR 0025 decision 5, made a requirement by ADR 0028) | P9.0 |
| A call that was up when a device rebooted does not resume by itself | independent design (ADR 0028) | P9.0 |
