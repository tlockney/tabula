# P9.1: Intercom protocol and mock device (signaling)

| Behavior | Source | Packet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A tablet that can take part in a call says so when it registers, separately for hearing (`intercom_listen`) and for speaking (`intercom_talk`), so a tablet whose microphone is unavailable still receives calls and broadcasts and is still a call target | independent design, ADR 0025 decision 7 | P9.1 |
| HA can ring one tablet, or start a one-way broadcast on it, naming who is calling and offering either a two-way call or a broadcast; a tablet that cannot hear audio is never rung | independent design, ADR 0025 decisions 1, 2 | P9.1 |
| A ringing tablet reports that it is ringing, a tablet in a call reports that it is in one, and a tablet sending a broadcast reports that instead, so HA's intercom sensor distinguishes ringing from a call in progress without polling | independent design, ADR 0025 decision 5 | P9.1 |
| An unanswered call stops ringing after the ring limit and reports why it ended, so a call into an empty room is over within a minute and HA can raise a missed-call notification | independent design, ADR 0025 decision 1, ADR 0028 guard 2 | P9.1 |
| Every call ends by itself at the hard call limit even if nobody hangs up, and says so, so feedback between two tablets cannot run unattended | independent design, ADR 0028 guards 2, 3 | P9.1 |
| HA can end a call on any tablet in any call state and the tablet stops without a person touching it, including a ringing or connecting call | independent design, ADR 0028 guard 3 | P9.1 |
| A broadcast is live and one-way: the receiving tablet plays it without being answered, and its microphone stays closed | independent design, ADR 0025 decision 2 | P9.1 |
| The two halves of a call exchange their own connection details through HA without HA reading or rewriting them, so the audio still goes directly between the tablets | independent design, ADR 0025 decisions 3, 4 | P9.1 |
| A call that was up or ringing when a tablet lost its link or restarted is gone when it comes back and reports the disconnect, rather than resuming by itself | independent design, ADR 0028 guard 4 | P9.1 |
| A call can be started from the tablet's own screen by naming a device, an area or a broadcast, and HA decides who is called | independent design, ADR 0025 decision 6 | P9.1 |
| HA can follow a call as it happens: it is told when the call is answered, declined, or ended and why, so automations can react to a missed or finished call | independent design, ADR 0025 decision 5 | P9.1 |
