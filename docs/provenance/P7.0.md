# P7.0: Voice device spike

| Behavior | Source | Packet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice capture is offered only on a device type whose microphone passed the P7.0 measurements: the grant can be restored without a person after a reboot and after a Wi-Fi reconnect, a foreground service keeps capturing with another app in front and with the panel off, and continuous wake-word detection stays well inside the idle CPU target. A device type that fails gets no voice, and stays fully working without it | F-KSK-21, ADR 0024 item 1, ADR 0029 | P7.0 |
| On Fire OS the microphone grant may be withdrawn at boot and after a Wi-Fi reconnect and then has to be applied again (`pm grant`, the app's grant screen, or the provisioning script); until it is, the device reports no capture rather than a silent stream of zeros, so a listener reads as inactive instead of pretending to hear | F-KSK-21, ADR 0029 | P7.0 |
| The device stops listening while its own reply plays and marks itself unavailable for listening until the reply ends, because the tablet has no echo canceller that Fire OS exposes; a wake word spoken during a reply is not heard | F-KSK-21, ADR 0024, ADR 0029 item 3 | P7.0 |
| Audio device checks in `tools/device/` refuse to run unless the owner has scheduled the session (`TABULA_AUDIO_OK=1`), because they make a device listen and speak where people live | independent design | P7.0 |
